## Instructions for Facilitator

- Ask all **core questions** (marked ★). Probing questions are optional follow-ups.
- Do NOT lead the participant or suggest answers.
- Record key phrases verbatim where possible — do not paraphrase on the spot.
- If the participant gives a short answer, use neutral probes: *"Can you tell me more?"* / *"What do you mean by that?"* / *"Can you give an example?"*
