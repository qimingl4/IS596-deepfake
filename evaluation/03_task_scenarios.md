## Facilitator Script — Before Tasks Begin

*Read aloud to participant:*

> "I'm going to ask you to use the Deep-Guard Agent system to analyze three short video clips. For each video, your goal is to use the system to determine whether the video is authentic or a deepfake, and to understand the system's reasoning. Please think aloud as you work — tell me what you're seeing, what you're thinking, and any questions that come to mind. I won't be able to help you during the tasks, but I'm here to observe. There are no right or wrong answers — we're evaluating the system, not you. Do you have any questions before we begin?"

---

## System Introduction (2 minutes — READ ALOUD, NO DEMO)

> "Deep-Guard Agent analyzes the audio and visual features of a video to detect inconsistencies between lip movements and speech. You upload a video, click Analyze, and the system will generate a report with its findings. There are two report tabs: 'Report' shows the technical analysis, and 'Legal Report' shows the legal forensic documentation."

> "Please open the browser now — the system is already running at http://localhost:7860"
